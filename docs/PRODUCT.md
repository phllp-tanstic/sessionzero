# Product

## Thesis

Session Zero is the period in which the reference U.S. cash market is closed while markets capable
of expressing information about that equity continue trading.

SessionZero is intended for active crypto-native equity traders and quantitative traders using
24/7 tokenized U.S. equities who need to assess off-session repricing before the U.S. cash market
reopens. A price difference is not alpha until the system can explain the market state that
produced it.

## Positioning

- Primary track: **Alpha Factory**
- Submission direction: **Open Theme**
- Product category: **closed-market price-discovery intelligence**

## Phase 0 boundary

The current product is a verified data and service foundation only. It reports provider
capabilities and discovered Reality instruments. It produces no price forecast, market state,
confidence, gap, conviction, recommendation, backtest metric, or order.

The future states remain locked as `DISCOVERY`, `UNDERREACTION`, `OVERSHOOT`, and `NOISE`; future
decisions remain `LONG`, `SHORT`, `WAIT`, and `ABSTAIN`. None is implemented in Phase 0.
