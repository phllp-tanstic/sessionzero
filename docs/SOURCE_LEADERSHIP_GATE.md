# Independent second-source gate v1 — specification and evidence audit

Decision: **SOURCE_LEADERSHIP_SECOND_SOURCE_GATED**; therefore **SOURCE_LEADERSHIP_RESEARCH_BLOCKED**. This is a documentation/repository audit, not a provider integration, account-entitlement test, licensing opinion, or live market-data verification. The accepted 21-symbol research archive contains one contemporaneous continuous path (Bitget Reality 1H) plus later Alpaca SIP/raw cash boundaries. A second API endpoint is not automatically a second market. No leadership weights or State/Fair Value model were computed.

## Acceptance criteria for a genuinely independent source

A candidate passes only after *all* of these gates are evidenced for the accepted cohort and the declared prospective slots:

1. **Price formation:** the observation comes from independently executable orders/trades or a venue-specific order book, not a restatement of Reality, the same downstream composite, an indicative vendor mark, or a delayed copy of the cash outcome. Identify the venue, feed construction and any underlying index/constituents. A distinct provider or instrument name alone is insufficient.
2. **Temporal overlap:** timestamped observations exist during the cash-closed slots where Reality is eligible; the schedule and source closures are known. Distinguish event time, bar completion, provider publication, request start and local ingestion. Historical bars alone cannot certify original as-known latency.
3. **Granularity and depth:** a directly observed intraday path with enough non-missing overlapping slots and prospectively retained revisions; daily adjusted bars do not qualify. Symbol mapping must link each Reality instrument to the correct native/derivative security, including corporate-action/ticker-history caveats. Declare a fixed minimum coverage and precision before testing leadership, not after seeing correlations.
4. **Point-in-time capture:** immutable raw responses, first-ingestion and correction versions, explicit missing/closed/unknown status, and snapshots frozen before each T. A delayed feed may be used only at its actual known time; backdated event stamps cannot upgrade it.
5. **Rights and operations:** account entitlement, private research/internal backtesting/prospective storage rights and intended public derived-output rights are separately verified in writing or authoritative terms. Credentials, if needed, remain server-side read-only and never enter a public response or repository.
6. **Independence validation:** compare constituent/source documentation and paired raw venue observations. Test whether the candidate is mechanically copied from Reality or a shared index before any lagged explanatory or leadership study. High correlation is expected for one underlying stock and is **not** proof of copying; a different quote is **not** proof of independent formation. A later leadership experiment must use chronological, matched, predeclared evaluation with clustered uncertainty and report unavailable symbols honestly.

Passing technical access without overlap, PIT or rights leaves the source gated. The acceptance condition is a *verified independent aligned panel*, not an aesthetically plausible source chart.

## Independence findings and exact gate

**BOATS is the strongest independent-market hypothesis** because venue-specific Blue Ocean ATS executions are conceptually distinct from a Bitget Reality rToken market. It is **not ready**: the current credentials' BOATS entitlement, real-time stream availability, 21-symbol overlap, as-known capture timing, revisions, mapping/rights and public-derived permission have not been demonstrated. A delayed historical BOATS bar at event time E is known no earlier than its actual request/ingestion time; it cannot be inserted into an earlier slot's feature snapshot. Its eventual leadership value is a hypothesis, not a finding.

Bitget Stock+ might expose useful native overnight values but the trading venue/feed composition is not established; it could relay cash/overnight markets. Bitget MCP daily adjusted native data lacks the timing to test off-session leadership. A Bitget stock perpetual **last trade** may represent a separate executable derivative market, but price formation may be driven by the same index and underlying hedges, and no aligned accepted panel exists. Its *index/mark* is derived and does not pass independent-source acceptance by itself. Separate APIs, symbols, or quote currencies do not clear this gate.

To unblock BOATS, obtain **non-trading, read-only** confirmation of account/plan entitlement and project-specific rights (private research, internal backtesting, prospective raw retention, public derived outputs); then authorize a separate bounded technical verification of BOATS feed identity, timestamps, latency, 21-symbol coverage, raw revision behavior and mapping. None was requested or performed in this task. If those fail, revisit the stock-perpetual *trade/market* leg with the same independent-formation and PIT tests; do not substitute its composite index. No source is approved for Dynamic Source Leadership today.
